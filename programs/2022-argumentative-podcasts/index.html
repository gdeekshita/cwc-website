Argumentative Podcasts

The Immortality Podcast

By: Amy Ashish

In "Avengers," Thanos believed that overpopulation and limited resources would lead to the universe's destruction, a notion that prompts reflection on the consequences of human selfishness. This podcast explores the profound implications of eternal human existence, questioning whether Earth can sustain it and pondering the feasibility of relocating to Mars with the necessary resources and technology.

We are Killing the Planet

By: Annika Jivrajani

Have you ever wondered what problems new and modernized inventions are causing these days? Well, in this podcast we talk about things like social media, environmental problems, and pollution. So, if you want to know about any of the following topics make sure to tune in!

Let's Get Crafty!

By: Sonia Nikhil

Art is about putting your thoughts and feelings into a masterpiece. It is about embracing your inner creativity. Artificial Intelligent developed art takes away the factor of expressing your self. In this podcast, we will be conversing on how AI art has made an impact on the community. We will be especially talking about how you can make a difference in the art world!

The Robot Thieves

By: Jasveet Kaur

From robots stealing jobs to them controlling us. From robots controlling us to a virtual reality life. Why? Why all this so-called improvement of our world? We aren’t even improving it, we are inventing the very thing that will destroy us, and eventually the world. This podcast is about how robots are turning into humans and how they will TAKE OVER humans. If you really need examples for this, then here you go!

Wonder of Animals

By: Mohnish Amarnath Premnath

This podcast refers to all the animals of the world. Some people eat meat. Have you ever wondered how the meat is coming to your house ? People kidnap animals and lock them in cages. Then they kill them and give out meat. The hair and teeth and claws or talons are used for making jewelry. Some people decide to kill animals for no reason. Hear “The Wonders of Animals” podcast and try to help the animals, as they did to us.

Helping the Shelter

By: Avnika Nandini Prakash

Have you ever wondered what it's like to see the eyes of a powerful little puppy? Or a calm little kitten? Well, your in luck, 'cause this podcast will answer your questions!! Stay tuned to hear scary stuff, memories, and more and more on Helping the Shelter!!
