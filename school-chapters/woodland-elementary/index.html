Woodland Elementary

Coming soon

Launching October 2026

Woodland Elementary is getting its own CWC chapter: a student-led writing club for young writers in grades 3–5.

What families can expect

  • Story-driven workshops. Themed units, such as fractured fairy tales, spooky stories and adventure tales, after school each week.
  • High school ambassadors. Sessions are led by trained high school ambassadors who love writing, supported by a teacher at the school.
  • Printed workbooks. Brainstorming exercises and step-by-step guides help every student go from idea to finished story.
  • A keepsake magazine. At the end of the program, every student's writing is published in a CWC magazine.

Get notified when sign-ups open

Email us and we will let you know as soon as the Woodland chapter is ready to welcome students.
